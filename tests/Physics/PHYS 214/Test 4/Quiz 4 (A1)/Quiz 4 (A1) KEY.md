## EM Induction (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** What does Lenz's law state about the direction of an induced current?

(a) It enhances the change in flux  
(b) It is independent of flux changes  
(c) It always flows clockwise  
(d) It opposes the change in flux that produced it  

**Answer:** (d)  
**Explanation:** Lenz's law says the induced current creates a magnetic effect that opposes the change in magnetic flux, consistent with energy conservation.  
**Resources:** OpenStax Univ. Phys. Vol. 2 – 13.1 Faraday’s Law: https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/13-1-faradays-law  

---

**Q2.** According to Faraday's law, the magnitude of the induced emf in a loop is proportional to:

(a) The magnetic field strength only  
(b) The loop resistance  
(c) The rate of change of magnetic flux  
(d) The loop circumference  

**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** Faraday's law gives $\lvert \mathcal{E} \rvert = \left| d\Phi_B/dt \right|$, so the emf depends on how fast the flux changes.  
**Resources:** Same as Q1

**Q3.** Which expression correctly defines magnetic flux through a flat loop area $A$ in field $B$ at angle $\theta$?

(a) $\Phi_B = BA$  
(b) $\Phi_B = B/A$  
(c) $\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta$  
(d) $\Phi_B = BA\sin\theta$  

**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** Magnetic flux is $\Phi_B = \vec B \cdot \vec A = BA\cos\theta$.  
**Resources:** Same as Q1

**Q4.** In motional emf, charges in a conductor moving with velocity $\vec v$ through field $\vec B$ are separated by which force?

(a) $q\vec E$  
(b) Gravity  
(c) $q\vec v \times \vec B$  
(d) Nuclear force  

**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** The magnetic part of the Lorentz force drives charge separation and produces the emf.  
**Resources:** Same as Q1

**Q5.** A loop enters a region of increasing magnetic field into the page. To oppose the change, the induced current's own magnetic field points:

(a) Into the page  
(b) Out of the page  
(c) Horizontally  
(d) It is zero  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** The increasing into-page flux is opposed by an induced field out of the page (Lenz's law).

**Q6.** The negative sign in Faraday's law $\mathcal{E} = -\,d\Phi_B/dt$ expresses:

(a) A meaningless sign convention

(c) Lenz's law: induced emf opposes flux changes

**Resources:** Same as Q1

(b) Loops of induced current in bulk conductors

## LR Circuits & Inductors (Q11–Q20)

**Q11.** In an RL circuit, the time constant $\tau$ is:

(a) $R/L$  
(b) $L/R$  
(c) $RC$  
(d) $1/RC$  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** For an RL circuit $\tau = L/R$, which sets how fast current approaches steady state.  
**Resources:** OpenStax Vol. 2 – 14.4 RL Circuits: https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/14-4-rl-circuits

**Q12.** Immediately after an RL circuit is connected to a DC source, the ideal inductor:

(a) Allows full current instantly  
(b) Opposes any change in current, acting like an open circuit  
(c) Behaves like a capacitor  
(d) Has no effect  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** An inductor resists sudden change in current, so initial current is limited.  
**Resources:** Same as Q11

**Q13.** Long after connection to a DC source, the ideal inductor in an RL circuit behaves like:

(a) An open circuit

**Explanation:** Once current is steady, $dI/dt = 0$ so the inductor's voltage drop is zero.

(a) Positive  
(b) Negative

(a) Minimum

## Maxwell’s Equations & EM Waves (Q41–Q50)

**Q41.** Gauss's law for magnetism states:

(a) $\nabla\cdot\vec B = \mu_0\rho_m$  
(b) Net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero  
(c) Magnetic field lines start on magnetic charges  
(d) Magnetic field is always uniform  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** There are no isolated magnetic monopoles; flux in equals flux out.  
**Resources:** OpenStax Vol. 2 – 16.1  

---

**Q42.** In vacuum, the speed of light is:

(a) $c = \mu_0\epsilon_0$  
(b) $c = 1/(\mu_0\epsilon_0)$  
(c) $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$  
(d) $c = \sqrt{\mu_0/\epsilon_0}$  

**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** Maxwell showed EM waves propagate at $c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}$.  

---

**Q43.** In an electromagnetic plane wave:

(a) $\vec E \parallel \vec B \parallel$ propagation  
(b) $\vec E \perp \vec B$ and both $\perp$ propagation  
(c) $\vec E$ only along propagation  
(d) $\vec B$ only along propagation  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** EM waves are transverse: $E$, $B$, and direction are mutually perpendicular.

**Q44.** The displacement current term was added to Ampere's law to:

(a) Change Gauss's law

(c) Ensure consistency with charge conservation in time-varying fields

**Answer:** (c)  
**Explanation:** It allows Ampere–Maxwell law to hold where electric fields change but no conduction current flows.

**Q45.** The Poynting vector $\vec S$ represents:

(a) Charge density  
(b) Energy flux (power per unit area) of EM waves

**Explanation:** $\vec S$ points in the direction of energy transport.

## Reflection & Refraction (Q51–Q60)

**Q51.** Snell's law is:

(a) $n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$  
(b) $F = ma$  
(c) $V = IR$  
(d) $pV = nRT$  

**Answer:** (a)  
**Explanation:** It relates angles of incidence and refraction via indices of refraction.  
**Resources:** OpenStax Vol. 3 – 1.3 Refraction  

---

**Q52.** When light enters a medium with higher $n$, it:

(a) Speeds up and bends away from the normal  
(b) Slows down and bends toward the normal  
(c) Slows down and bends away from the normal  
(d) Is unchanged  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** Higher index means lower speed and bending toward the normal.  

---

**Q53.** Total internal reflection occurs when light:

(a) Goes from low $n$ to high $n$  
(b) Goes from high $n$ to low $n$ with $\theta > \theta_c$  
(c) Strikes any mirror  
(d) Travels in vacuum  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** Above the critical angle, all light reflects back into the denser medium.  

---

**Q54.** For a plane mirror, the angle of reflection equals:

(a) Angle of refraction  
(b) Angle of incidence  
(c) Critical angle  
(d) $90^\circ$  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** Law of reflection: $\theta_r = \theta_i$.  

---

**Q55.** The index of refraction is:

(a) $n = v/c$  
(b) $n = c/v$  
(c) $n = 1/v$  
(d) $n = c$  

**Answer:** (b)  
**Explanation:** $n$ compares light speed in vacuum to that in the medium.

**Q56.** If $n_2 < n_1$, the refracted ray:

(a) Unpolarized

**Answer:** (c)
